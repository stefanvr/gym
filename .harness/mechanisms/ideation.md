# Ideation

Generates candidate ways forward when exploration has established a problem but has not produced a satisfactory solution.

Ideation is an optional Brainstorm mechanism, not a required phase.

## Trigger

Use when:

- the problem or tension is sufficiently understood
- existing material does not already provide the answer
- the owner does not have a settled direction
- progress is blocked by lack of plausible alternatives

Do not invoke merely because more ideas could be generated.

## Purpose

Produce useful candidates that can be compared, challenged, or taken into downstream decision work.

Ideation does not decide.

## Method

1. Restate the problem or tension being solved.
2. Identify constraints already established by evidence or owner intent.
3. Generate a small set of meaningfully different approaches.
4. Prefer differences in underlying approach over superficial variations.
5. Explain the important trade-off of each candidate.
6. Recommend when there is a clear reason to do so.
7. Keep every generated option marked as a harness suggestion until accepted or adopted by an owning skill.

## Scrutiny

Ask:

- Are we solving an established problem, or inventing one?
- Are the candidates genuinely different?
- Does any option violate known constraints?
- Are we treating a generated idea as owner intent?
- Are we prematurely making a Domain, App, Style, or Tech decision?
- Would another round of evidence gathering be more useful than generating alternatives?

## Completion

Ideation is complete when there are enough distinct, plausible candidates to unblock discussion or downstream decision-making.

More candidates are not inherently better.

## Output

Ideation produces:

- suggested alternatives
- trade-offs
- recommendation, when justified
- unresolved questions

All remain draft until adopted elsewhere.

## Invariants

- ideation is optional
- ideation starts from an understood problem
- generated ideas remain suggestions
- quantity is not the goal
- do not hide trade-offs
- do not turn candidate generation into a decision
- do not manufacture owner preference
