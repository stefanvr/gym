# Brainstorm

Non-authoritative exploration surface for supplied material and draft owner-intent work.

## Purpose

Produce useful draft material so later owning capabilities can make explicit decisions without inventing missing intent.

When the Interview Me method is active, use it for reusable general owner-intent elicitation. Brainstorm Analyze remains the direct route for analysing supplied material.

## Owns

`doc/brainstorm/` only as **temporary local working material** when an investigation must survive context loss, through the narrower ownership declared by its skills. Brainstorm is conversational/transient by default; this path is Git-ignored and is never Project state. Brainstorm artifacts never become current authority merely by existing and are not edited just to mirror later decisions.

First-class method packs use their own non-authoritative working paths and do not share this ownership.

## Skills

- `interview` — direct Brainstorm owner-intent exploration; active Interview Me is the general reusable elicitation method
- `analyze` — analyze supplied material

## Shared guides and mechanisms

- Method Pack Contract defines the method boundary
- Grill for deliberate pressure-testing of consequential candidates before authority
- Provenance owns evidence/provenance classes and promotion rules.
- Ideation may be used when exploration is stuck.
- Authorship Stance governs who is supplying intent where relevant.
- Routing sends discovered goals/decisions to their owning capability.

## Dependencies

Consumes owner/source material and may route discovered goals or decisions to Goal, Domain, App, Style, or Tech. No downstream capability treats Brainstorm output as authority.

## Invariants

- brainstorm output never becomes authority by implication
- Brainstorm is transient by default; persisted working material is local/ignored and exists only when useful for temporary continuity
- the Provenance mechanism applies to Brainstorm findings
- owner intent is never invented
- tensions and missing answers remain explicit rather than being silently resolved
- specifications, technology choices, and Goal intent are not owned here
- method activation does not transfer ownership of Brainstorm working material
