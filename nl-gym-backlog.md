# NL Climbing & Bouldering Gym Backlog

A sweep of climbing and bouldering gyms in the Netherlands, compiled from the
NKBV overview, Resole's gym directories, and a handful of other aggregator
sites (Climbingspot, BoulderBazen, POF!ZAK, Stripe Away). Entries already
logged in [`gyms/`](gyms/) are ~~struck through~~ — everything else is a
candidate for a new `gyms/*.md` file.

Scope: real indoor climbing/bouldering gyms only. Outdoor high-ropes/tree
courses (Klimbos, Fun Forest, Adventure Valley-style parks) are left out —
different category from a route-set wall. Known-closed or cancelled projects
(Monk Club Amsterdam, Nomad Boulders Amsterdam, Graviton Sittard, Beest
Boulders Leiden, Boulderwerk Gouda, Fox Bouldergym Zeeland, KlimAvontuur
Middelburg) are omitted entirely.

Sources: [NKBV](https://nkbv.nl/kenniscentrum/klimmen-in-nederland.html) ·

## Notes for filling these in

- Zeeland currently has no indoor climbing/bouldering gym (KlimAvontuur
  Middelburg closed; Fox Bouldergym never opened) — nothing to add there yet.
- Drenthe's first boulder gym, Huneboulder in Assen, is very recent — worth
  double-checking it's actually open before logging it as `visited`/`checked`.
- A few names above are genuinely uncertain and worth verifying before
  writing a `gyms/*.md` entry: whether "Klimcentrum Alkmaar" and "Boulderhal
  Sendmast" are the same venue, and whether Arnhem's "RijnBoulder" is a
  distinct building from the existing Mountain Network Arnhem entries or the
  same one under another name.
