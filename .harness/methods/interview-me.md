# Interview Me

General elicitation method for extracting trustworthy owner intent without asking the owner to design Harness artifacts.

## Purpose

Use focused conversation to uncover intent, preferences, constraints, tensions, unknowns, and meaningful decision edges when an authority lacks enough reliable input.

Interview Me evolves the useful elicitation behavior of Brainstorm Interview into a reusable method that can be invoked by Domain, App, Style, Tech, Design, or Goal-oriented exploration without making Brainstorm an authority gateway.

## Inputs

Required:

- an area whose owner intent is materially incomplete
- access to the owner

Optional:

- current Goal
- current Project authority
- supplied references or examples
- existing working material
- known constraints

## Working outputs

Usually transient conversational understanding.

When durable notes materially help later reconciliation, Interview Me may create non-authoritative working notes under:

`doc/working/interviews/`

Use shared Provenance classes when recording findings. Notes may contain Said, Suggested and accepted, Open, tensions, free dimensions, constraints, and candidate decisions.

## Authority interaction

Interview Me owns no Project truth.

Route a reliable conclusion to the authority that owns it. That authority decides whether and how the conclusion enters its authoritative artifact.

Confirmation that an interpretation matches owner intent is provenance validation, not lifecycle approval.

## Invocation

Use when missing intent blocks or materially weakens an authority decision.

Do not use an interview as a mandatory ceremony when the owner has already supplied clear enough intent or the answer is observable from authoritative Project state.

## Method

- ask one meaningful question or tightly related thread at a time
- ask about the product, world, interaction, perception, or constraint rather than the destination document
- prefer concrete comparisons and grounded examples over blank-page abstractions
- clearly label Harness suggestions and wait for acceptance before treating them as owner intent
- follow high-information reactions and distinctions
- record genuine indifference as a free dimension rather than continuing to press
- preserve tensions instead of resolving them for neatness
- play back interpretations when attribution matters
- stop when additional questioning is unlikely to change the relevant authority decision

## Completion

Complete when the current decision has enough trustworthy owner signal to proceed, or when remaining uncertainty is explicit and cannot usefully be reduced by further interviewing now.

## Invariants

- never manufacture owner preference
- never convert an inference into something the owner supposedly said
- never require the owner to know Harness document structure
- never silently resolve tensions
- never promote interview notes directly into Project authority
- remain bounded by the current Goal or explicitly scoped exploration
