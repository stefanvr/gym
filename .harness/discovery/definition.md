# Discovery

Discovery is the non-authoritative space for intentionally investigating a question before deciding whether there is a Goal or Project change.

## Principle

Discovery helps the owner and agent decide; it does not decide on their behalf.

Discovery is **transient by default**. It owns no durable archive and is allowed to end with no action.

## What Discovery may use

Discovery may inspect the repository, active Project authority, native documentation, evidence, and relevant history. It may use Brainstorm, active methods, Grill, and installed Extensions when they improve the investigation.

## Outcomes

A Discovery has three legitimate dispositions:

- **close** — nothing worth retaining or doing
- **publish** — explicitly route a useful finding into existing Project state/documentation through its normal owner
- **promote** — explicitly turn one bounded actionable outcome into a Goal

Publication is not automatic. Project truth goes through the owning Project-reasoning authority; explanatory knowledge goes through Project Document/native documentation as appropriate. Discovery never creates a parallel `doc/discovery/` archive.

## Relationship to Dream

Discovery is question-driven: *what should we understand about this?*

Dream is experience-driven: *what reusable improvement does accumulated work suggest?*

Both are non-authoritative and require explicit promotion before changing Project or Harness authority.

## Relationship to Brainstorm

Discovery is the activity/context. Brainstorm is one reasoning capability usable inside it. Brainstorm working material remains temporary and does not become Project truth by persistence.

## Invariants

**[DISCOVERY-01]** Discovery is transient and non-authoritative by default; durable Project state exists only after explicit publication through an existing owner or promotion into a Goal.

- a Discovery may end without producing a Goal
- no backlog, priority, owner, estimate, status pipeline, or candidate-Goal queue is created here
- useful methods and Extensions may inform reasoning without gaining authority
- conversation or working material never becomes authority by implication
- actionable future work is not silently smuggled into Project state; it is promoted to a Goal
