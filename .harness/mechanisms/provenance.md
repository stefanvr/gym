# Provenance

Brainstorm findings always retain how they became known.

## Classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Said** | Directly stated by the owner. |
| **Suggested and accepted** | Proposed by the harness and explicitly accepted by the owner. |
| **Observed** | Directly evidenced by supplied material. |
| **Inferred** | Interpretation derived from evidence. |
| **Suggested** | New possibility introduced by the harness. |
| **Open** | Unknown, ambiguous, conflicting, unanswered, not yet explored, or explicitly free. |

## Rule

Never silently promote evidence:

```text
Observed ≠ Inferred
Inferred ≠ Said
Suggested ≠ Accepted
Accepted suggestion ≠ owner-originated idea
Open ≠ negative evidence
```

A later owning skill may turn draft evidence into a decision.

Brainstorm itself does not.
